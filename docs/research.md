# User Research

## 1. Research Objective
Understand how football fans access club information,
discover upcoming matches and explore ticket options.

## 2. Initial Target Audience
Club-connected football fans.

## 3. User Segments
- Dedicated club supporters
- Prospective match attendees

## 4. Initial User Profile
The Club-Connected Football Fan

## 5. Research Questions
- How do fans consume football news?
- How do fans discover fixtures?
- How do fans find match tickets?
- What information influences purchasing decisions?
- What difficulties occur during these activities?

## 6. Research Methodology
- Competitor usability audits
- Task-based usability observation
